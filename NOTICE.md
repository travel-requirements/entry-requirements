## What is and is not covered

The entry rules themselves are the rules of sovereign states. Nobody owns them,
and this licence makes no claim over them.

What is licensed here is the **compilation**: the schema, the
passport-by-destination matrix, the editorial text, and the source-and-verification
trail recorded against every fact. That is the work, and that is what the
attribution condition attaches to.

dataset and are licensed separately by their upstream project.

## No warranty

The dataset is provided as-is. Immigration rules change without notice, and the
final decision at any border rests with that country's authorities. Each record
names the official source it was compiled from and the date it was last checked
against that source; **the source is the authority, not this dataset**.

Do not use this data as the sole basis for a travel, boarding or admission
decision. See `caveats` in `/data/index.json` for what the data explicitly does

compiled and verified.
